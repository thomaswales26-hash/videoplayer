A couple of things to know.

System requirements

macOS 13 or later. One universal download supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Supported playback formats and 4K performance depend on your system.

MKV playback

Install FFmpeg separately with brew install ffmpeg. Video and audio conversion runs locally on your Mac. Discs must be mounted; this is file playback, not DVD or Blu-ray menu navigation. Encrypted Blu-ray playback isn't supported.
